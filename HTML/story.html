PLACES WITH A SOUL

We do not collect houses. We follow a feeling—the hush of a courtyard, the weight of old stone, the light that makes a place feel like yours.

Follow the story
EST. 2014
THE BEGINNING

A wrong turn,
a right feeling

The first Stackly home was not meant to be a business. It was a farmhouse at the end of a road that did not appear on any map, found by accident on a rain-soaked afternoon in Puglia.

We stayed for a week and left with a question: why did so many beautiful houses feel so anonymous, while this one felt like it had been waiting for us? The answer became our work.

We look for the feeling first. The floor plan can wait.
What we believe
OUR BELIEFS

The things we
refuse to compromise

Four beliefs shape every home we choose and every stay we host. They are not policies. They are instincts we have learned to trust.

01

Architecture with memory

A building should know where it stands. We choose homes that belong to their landscape, not ones that ignore it.

02

The table is the heart

Every home has a kitchen worth cooking in and a table worth lingering at. Food is how a place introduces itself.

03

Privacy is a luxury

The best mornings are the ones no one else sees. We protect the silence, the view, and the freedom to do nothing.

04

Local before polished

We would rather have a slightly imperfect room with a story than a flawless one with no sense of place.

Hover a belief to bring it forward
A DECADE IN MOTION

Slow growth,
careful choices

2014
THE FIRST HOME

One farmhouse in Puglia

A single stone house, a borrowed bicycle, and a guest book that filled faster than we could print new pages.

2017
THE FIRST TEAM

Hosts, not managers

We stopped outsourcing the welcome. Every home gained a named host who knew the neighbours, the baker, and the best quiet beach.

2021
THE COLLECTION

Thirty homes, one standard

The collection grew slowly. For every home we said yes to, we said no to a dozen. The standard never moved.

2026
TODAY

A quieter kind of luxury

More places, more people, and the same belief: a great stay should feel personal, unhurried, and a little bit inevitable.

Stackly began with two people who could not walk past an open door without wondering what was inside.

DESIGN ETHOS

Designed to
disappear into place

The best design does not announce itself. It shows up in the coolness of a stone floor at noon, the width of a doorway, the place your book lands at the end of the day.

We look for homes where the architecture and the landscape are in quiet conversation—and then we leave them alone.

THE PROCESS

How a house
becomes a home

Four quiet steps stand between a beautiful property and a Stackly home. None of them can be rushed.

01
LISTEN

We start with the feeling

Before floor plans and rates, we ask what kind of holiday you are trying to have.

02
VISIT

We stay before you do

Every home is slept in, cooked in, and walked through at different times of day.

03
REFINE

We fix the small things

The lamp that is too bright. The chair that is too low. The welcome that arrives too late.

04
WELCOME

We hand you the key

Then we step back. Your host is close enough to help, and far enough to disappear.
